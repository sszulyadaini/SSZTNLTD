PROJECT OVERVIEW

A founder project
in the Sidra ecosystem.

The SidraStart listing describes HALAL EXCHANGE as a global Shari’ah-compliant tokens exchange intended to support the trading of Halal World Coin and other digital assets. Its stated vision is to connect Islamic finance principles with digital trading and to support financial inclusion.

This summary reflects the project’s published description and proposed aims, not a verification that the exchange is operational, that transactions meet particular standards, or that any security measures have been independently assessed. The listing identifies the project as part of the Sidra ecosystem; it does not establish specific SidraChain implementation details.

THE PROJECT BRIEF

Problem, proposed response
and a roadmap to explore.

01 / PROBLEM DESCRIBED

Access and values

The listing identifies a gap for people and businesses seeking accessible digital-asset exchange options aligned with Islamic financial principles. It notes concerns about the suitability and accessibility of existing platforms.

02 / PROPOSED RESPONSE

Ethical digital trading

The project proposes a digital exchange focused on transparent, accessible trading aligned with its stated Shari’ah-compliance goal. This is the proposal in the listing, not confirmation of a working platform or verified compliance.

03 / DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

Phases listed on SidraStart

The listing outlines five planned phases: initiation and research; platform development; halal certification partnerships and merchant onboarding; marketing and launch; and user feedback and iteration. Completion of any phase is not verified here.

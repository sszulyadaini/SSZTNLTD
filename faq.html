FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers
to start with.

A few notes about service enquiries and working together. Specific arrangements depend on the project and written agreement.

FAQ — 08Still have a question? Get in touch. ↘
What services can I ask about?+

You can ask about Web3 and blockchain, AI, cybersecurity, web development, digital marketing, mobile technology, international e-commerce, and the other service areas on the Services page.

How much do services cost?+

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the agreed work, requirements, dependencies and responsibilities. A price or quote is not confirmed until provided and accepted in writing.

How long will a project take?+

Timing depends on scope, availability, decisions, content, third parties and other project dependencies. No delivery date is guaranteed until agreed in writing.

Do you have a large team available?+

This website does not represent a large in-house team. The founder discusses each request directly, and any delivery capacity, collaborators, roles and availability must be confirmed before a commitment is made.

How do you handle security work?+

Security discussions are defensive and scoped. A security assessment requires the system owner’s written authorisation and clear limits. This website does not promise certification, compliance or complete security. See the cybersecurity service page.

Can I share passwords or sensitive files in a request?+

No. Do not send passwords, account credentials, payment information, confidential customer data or other sensitive information through the website enquiry forms or an unrequested email draft.

Does submitting a website form send information to you?+

No server submission is implemented. After validation, the form opens a draft in your email application. Your information is not sent until you review and send that email yourself. The intended company recipient address is still a placeholder.

When is a project accepted?+

An enquiry is not an acceptance or contract. The parties must separately agree scope, capacity, deliverables, pricing, timeline, responsibilities and terms before work begins.

Do you provide ongoing support?+

Support or maintenance can be discussed for a particular project. Availability, scope, response expectations and fees must be agreed separately; no service level is promised by this website.

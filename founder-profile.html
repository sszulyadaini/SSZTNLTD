SULAIMAN SALIHU ZULYADAINI

Connecting business needs with useful technology.

Sulaiman Salihu Zulyadaini is the Founder and CEO of S S ZULYADAINI TECHNOVA LTD. His interests bring together business relationships, entrepreneurship and the practical use of digital technology.

As a Business Relationship Manager at Moniepoint Microfinance Bank, Sulaiman works in a role shaped by understanding customer needs and building professional relationships. This experience informs how he approaches technology ideas: begin by listening, clarify the problem and consider solutions in the context of the people and business they are meant to support. His employer is separate from S S ZULYADAINI TECHNOVA LTD; this profile does not imply endorsement or a business relationship between the organisations.

Earlier, he worked as an IT Support Intern at the Bauchi State ICT Agency. He is currently studying for a B.Sc. in Computer Science at the National Open University of Nigeria (NOUN); the degree is in progress.

Sulaiman is interested in cybersecurity, web development, AI, Web3, blockchain, e-commerce, digital marketing and fintech, alongside business development and customer relationships. These are stated interests and learning areas, not claims of specialist credentials or certifications.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Service
questions.

Are all services available as a ready-made package?+

No. Each enquiry is discussed and scoped based on the need, capacity, requirements and responsibilities. No specific delivery team or package is implied.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

Pricing and timelines are not fixed on this website. They can only be discussed after an initial conversation and a mutually agreed scope. No work begins until the terms are confirmed.

Can you work with our existing team?+

Potential collaboration can be discussed. Roles, availability, communication and deliverables must be agreed for each project.

What do you need to assess a request?+

A short description of the goal, current situation, audience, important constraints and desired timing is a helpful start. Do not include passwords, confidential data or sensitive personal information in an enquiry.

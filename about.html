03 / FOUNDER PROFILE

Business understanding.
Technology curiosity.

Sulaiman Salihu Zulyadaini is the Founder and CEO of S S ZULYADAINI TECHNOVA LTD, a business and technology entrepreneur with a professional background in business relationship management.

In his current role as a Business Relationship Manager at Moniepoint Microfinance Bank, he works at the intersection of business needs and customer relationships. That experience informs his interest in building useful technology solutions: start by understanding people, communicate clearly, and connect the right ideas to real business needs.

His earlier experience as an IT Support Intern at the Bauchi State ICT Agency gave him a practical introduction to technology support. He is currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science at the National Open University of Nigeria (NOUN); the degree is in progress.

As a founder, Sulaiman is interested in entrepreneurship, business development, digital innovation and solutions that help organisations improve their operations, reach customers and grow. His technical interests include cybersecurity, web development, AI, Web3, blockchain, e-commerce, digital marketing and fintech. These are areas of interest and continued learning—not a claim of certifications or specialist credentials.

This notice describes the current static website implementation. Hosting, business email handling, and retention arrangements should be confirmed before launch and updated here if they change.

LAST REVIEWED: 26 OCTOBER 2026

Who is responsible?

This website is for S S ZULYADAINI TECHNOVA LTD. The company’s registered office, privacy contact and data-protection contact have not been supplied. Add the correct contact details before publishing a final operational privacy notice.

Information entered in an enquiry or feedback form

The contact, service-request and feedback forms run in your browser. They validate the fields you enter and prepare an email draft using your local email application. The website code does not send that information to a form-processing server or store it in a website database. Your details are only transmitted if you choose to send the email. The company recipient address is currently empty in the business configuration and must be supplied before enquiries can be sent to the company.

If you send an enquiry or feedback, your email provider and the recipient’s email provider may process and retain the message under their own terms. Once the company inbox and its operational handling are configured, the company may process the information to review and respond. Feedback is not automatically published; testimonials require review and separate permission. Do not include credentials, payment details, special-category personal data or confidential third-party information in a general enquiry.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

The current website code does not install analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or a consent-management cookie. It does not intentionally create browser storage for marketing or analytics. This should be rechecked if third-party scripts, embeds or new hosting features are added.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is delivered by its hosting provider. Hosting infrastructure may process technical connection data, such as IP address, request time and requested resource, to operate and secure the service. The exact logs, retention period, hosting location and provider depend on the production hosting configuration, which is not specified here. Confirm those details with the hosting provider and update this notice before launch.

Retention and your choices

This website does not retain enquiry form submissions. If you choose to send an email, the message may remain in your sent folder, mail provider systems and the company’s inbox. The company’s email-retention period and deletion procedure have not been specified. Contact the company using its verified privacy contact to ask about an enquiry once that contact is published.

Third-party services

The current published code uses first-party HTML, CSS and JavaScript and does not embed social feeds, maps, video players or analytics. Third-party infrastructure used by the production host and the email services you choose are outside this site code and should be identified here if their configuration becomes known.

Changes

Update this notice whenever the form, hosting, analytics, email, data retention or contact arrangements change. This page is a description of the current implementation, not a substitute for legal advice or a jurisdiction-specific compliance review.

Before production: confirm the hosting provider and log retention, official privacy contact, email recipient, email retention practices and applicable legal requirements.
